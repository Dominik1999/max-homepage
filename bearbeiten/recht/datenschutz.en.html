Protecting your personal data is important to me. Below I explain how personal data are processed when you visit this website.

2. General information on data processing

I process personal data only to the extent necessary to provide this website and my services, or where you have given your consent. The legal bases are, in particular, Art. 6 (1) (a) (consent), (b) (contract / steps prior to entering into a contract) and (f) (legitimate interests) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR; in German: Datenschutz-Grundverordnung, DSGVO).

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen. When the pages are accessed, the server automatically processes technical data in so-called log files: IP address, date and time of access, page requested, volume of data transferred, referrer, and browser and operating system information.

The purpose is the secure and stable operation of the website. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with the hosting provider. The log files are deleted automatically after 7 days.

4. Fonts

The fonts used are delivered locally from my server. No fonts are loaded from external providers (e.g. Google Fonts); no connection to third-party servers is established and your IP address is not transmitted in the process.

5. Contacting me (form and email)

If you write to me via the contact form or by email, I process the data you provide — name, email address and the content of your message — in order to deal with your enquiry. The form is transmitted to me as an email. The mailbox kontakt@925law.de is also operated by Hetzner Online GmbH (see section 3); a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place for this as well.

After you submit the form, a short confirmation of receipt is sent automatically to the email address you provided. It addresses you by name but does not repeat the content of your message. Your enquiry is also transmitted to me together with the website language you selected and the date and time of submission.

To protect the form against abusively frequent submissions, the server stores your IP address in hashed form (as a checksum) together with the times of submission when you submit the form. These data are deleted automatically after 24 hours at the latest. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (protecting the form against abuse).

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where the enquiry is aimed at entering into or performing a client engagement (Mandat), and otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (interest in answering your enquiry). Your data are deleted as soon as the enquiry has been dealt with conclusively and no statutory retention obligations prevent deletion.

If an engagement comes about, further processing is governed by the statutory requirements for lawyers (including the lawyer’s duty of confidentiality and the retention periods under professional and commercial law).

Note on confidentiality: Messages sent via the contact form and ordinary emails are not end-to-end encrypted. It therefore cannot be ruled out that third parties may gain knowledge of their content in transit. For confidential or sensitive matters, I recommend that you call me (+49 30 75003173).

6. SSL/TLS encryption

To protect data in transit, this website uses SSL/TLS encryption, recognisable by “https://” in your browser’s address bar. It protects the transmission between your browser and the server but is not end-to-end encryption; messages sent via the contact form and by email are subject to the note on confidentiality in section 5.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access to the data processed about you (Art. 15)
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • erasure (Art. 17) and restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • data portability (Art. 20)
  • object to processing (Art. 21)
  • withdraw any consent you have given, with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3))

8. Right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit), Alt-Moabit 59-61, 10555 Berlin — datenschutz-berlin.de.

When things get serious

The stakes are high for you. For me, it’s familiar ground.

  • You’ve been handed a dismissal or a termination agreement. I build a strategy with you, challenge the dismissal and negotiate your severance. You have only three weeks: whoever doesn’t file has lost for certain, however unlawful the dismissal was.
  • You need a contract that holds up when it matters, or you have one that gives you a bad feeling. I draft it so it protects your interests and isn’t the first thing to buckle in a dispute.
  • Your job reference is worse than your work was, and it can cost you your next job. I decode the hidden phrasing and secure the assessment you’re entitled to, in court if it comes to that.
  • A client isn’t paying, and reminders stopped working long ago. I enforce your claim with real pressure, out of court and, if needed, before it.
  • You’re facing disciplinary proceedings, with your career on the line. I take the allegations apart, use every procedural flaw and fight for your career.
  • You have to let an employee go and don’t want to take any risks. I plan the separation watertight, so it holds and costly mistakes never arise.

About

I'm 925law — Max Englhard, attorney-at-law.

My career hasn’t run in a straight line: private practice, a fintech, the public sector, and most recently a large commercial firm. I’ve seen a lot and come to know both sides. I know what matters to a business, and just as well how it feels to be an employee.

What drives me is simple: I like helping people, and law is my tool for it. Most of my clients don’t care about the legal fine print — they want a solution. That’s what I’m good at: spotting the real problem and building a workable way through it, fast, that actually helps you. Clear advice, no runaround, no law-firm jargon, built around you.
